Privacy Policy

Applies to Nano, the personal WhatsApp assistant described in this build log. Last updated 17 September 2026.

Nano is a private household assistant, not a commercial service. It is operated by one person, on one personal computer, for that person's own family. It has no user accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, and no analytics. If you did not receive an invitation to message it, it is not for you.

Who operates it

Shlomo Garbi, an individual, operating Nano privately. Contact: github.com/Shlomog/family-ai-chief-of-staff/issues, or simply reply in the WhatsApp conversation with Nano.

What it collects

Only what you send it, and only when you message it first. Nano has no other source of information about you.

DataWhy it exists
Your WhatsApp phone number and display name Supplied by WhatsApp with every inbound message; used to know who is talking and to reply to the right conversation.
The content of the messages you send It is the request. Nano cannot answer a message it does not read.
Images, audio, documents and other media you attach Processed to answer the message they were attached to.
Message timestamps and delivery status Ordering, de-duplication, and not replying twice to the same message.

Nano does not collect location, contacts, device identifiers, browsing activity, or anything from WhatsApp conversations it is not part of.

Where it is stored

On a personal computer, in a local database file, protected by that machine's own disk encryption and account password. There is no hosted application server and no cloud database holding your messages. Nothing is copied to a data broker, an ad network, or an analytics service, because none are connected.

Who else sees it

  • Meta Platforms carries the messages, as it does for every WhatsApp conversation. Its handling is governed by the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic receives message text, and any attachments relevant to the request, in order to generate Nano's reply. Its handling is governed by the Anthropic Privacy Policy.
  • A service you explicitly ask for. If you ask Nano to add a calendar event or send an email, the necessary part of that request reaches that provider — because that is the thing you asked for.

Beyond those, nothing is shared, sold, rented, or disclosed — except where the law requires it.

How long it is kept

  • Media attachments are deleted automatically after 7 days by default.
  • Message history is kept locally for as long as the assistant is useful to the household, so it can remember context across conversations. It is deleted on request, and deleted in full whenever the database is reset.

Your choices

  • Stop. Stop messaging Nano and it stops receiving anything about you. It only ever writes into a conversation you already have with it, and it starts no conversation with anyone who has not written to it first.
  • Deletion. Ask — in the conversation, or through the contact link above — and your messages and media are deleted from the local database.
  • Access. Ask what is stored about you and you will be told.

Children

Nano is not directed at children under 13 and is not knowingly used by them. Where a minor in the operator's own family uses it, they do so with the knowledge and consent of their parent, who is the operator.

Security

Inbound webhook requests are verified against a signing secret, so messages that did not come from WhatsApp are rejected. Credentials are held outside the source code and are never committed to the public repository. No system is perfectly secure, and this one is a personal project run on personal hardware — treat it accordingly and do not send it anything you would not want stored on a family computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous wording remains visible in the public commit history of this repository.

Nano runs on NanoClaw. The full build is documented in the guide.
